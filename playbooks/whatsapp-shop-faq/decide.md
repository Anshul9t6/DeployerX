# Decide — WhatsApp shop FAQ

## Use when

- Same 10–20 questions repeat daily (price, stock, timing, delivery area)
- Owner already chats on WhatsApp
- Budget is near zero to low
- No engineer on staff

## Do NOT use when

- You need the bot to take payments or confirm orders without a human
- Prices change every hour with no written list
- Regulated advice (medical, legal, tax) is the main content
- Owner refuses to review answers for the first week
