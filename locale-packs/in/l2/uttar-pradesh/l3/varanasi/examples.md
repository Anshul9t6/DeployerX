# Varanasi — examples

## 1) Silk shop on WhatsApp

Owner gets 40+ messages/day: “Banarasi saree price?”, “Ready stock?”, “Ship to Delhi?”

**DeployerX path:** `whatsapp-shop-faq` + Hindi prompts + human approval for prices.

## 2) Guest house near ghats

Guests ask aarti time, check-in, AC room rate. Wrong answers destroy trust.

**Rule:** only answer from owner-approved FAQ sheet; else “Owner will confirm in 10 minutes.”

## 3) Coaching centre admissions

Seasonal burst of “fees?”, “batch timing?”, “demo class?”.

**DeployerX path:** FAQ playbook with Sheets as source of truth; lock dates/fees behind approval.
