# Code of conduct

## Expected

- No PII, phone numbers, or customer chat logs in the repo.
- Locale packs are **deltas**. Do not fork playbooks per city.
- Do not invent field-note Results or mark a pack `seeded` / `verified` from a template.
- Do not Path-A automate medical advice, credit, or religious instruction ([`guides/deployments-50.md`](guides/deployments-50.md) X rows).

## Not tolerated

Harassment, hate, or dumping scraped personal data into issues or PRs.
